# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel to analyze employee attrition, workforce trends, salary distribution, and department-wise performance insights.

This project helps HR teams and organizations make better workforce decisions through data visualization and analytics.

# 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides insights into employee attrition and workforce patterns using interactive charts, KPI cards, and filters.

The dashboard is designed to:
- Monitor employee attrition
- Analyze workforce demographics
- Understand salary and job role impact
- Support HR decision-making

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition trends
- Identify departments with high attrition
- Understand salary impact on attrition
- Compare employee distribution by age and education
- Create a clean and interactive HR dashboard

# 📈 Dashboard Insights

## 🔹 Attrition by Education
Shows employee attrition based on educational background such as:
- Life Sciences
- Marketing
- Medical
- Technical Degree

---

## 🔹 Attrition by Age
Analyzes attrition across different age groups.

### Observation:
- Highest attrition is seen in the **26–35** age group.

---

## 🔹 Attrition by Gender
Compares male and female attrition distribution.

---

## 🔹 Attrition based on Salary Slab
Displays employee attrition according to salary ranges:
- Upto 5k
- 5k–10k
- 10k–15k
- 15k+

---

## 🔹 Attrition by Year of Work
Shows attrition trends based on employee experience.

---

## 🔹 Attrition based on Job Role
Highlights job roles with higher attrition rates such as:
- Sales Executive
- Sales Representative
- Manager
